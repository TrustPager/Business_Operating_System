# Completeness Critique — Rulings the Synthesis Must Encode

## Contradiction rulings (C1-C10)

- **C1 (30-day CFA rule):** two tiers. Survival bar: 30-day cash ≥ CAC + COGS. CFA pass bar: 30-day gross profit > 2×(CAC+COGS) ("one customer funds two more"). 10x = aspiration. Diagnosis reports the tier, never a single pass/fail.
- **C2 (LTGP:CAC):** 3:1 = absolute never-scale-below floor; the touchpoint ladder (3:1 no humans / 6:1 one touchpoint / 9-12:1 two touchpoints) is the scaling gate. Most BOS shapes have two touchpoints → "aim ~6:1+ before aggressive spend." Hard-code gross profit (LTGP), never revenue-LTV.
- **C3 (Rule of 100 vs capacity):** gate first: "is the owner at delivery capacity?" Not at capacity + leads constraint → full Rule of 100. At capacity → Capacity Rule (price up, volume through systems, small protected owner ration: "the system does 100, the owner does 5"). At $0-10k/mo, delivery constraint is usually fake.
- **C4 (More-Better-New boundary):** creative, hooks, magnet, name, wrapper changes on an existing channel = BETTER. New platform or new Core Four cell = NEW. State explicitly. R1-A10's refresh order is the "Better" sub-sequence.
- **C5 (constraint list):** present a SIX-item rubric — leads, sales/conversion, delivery/capacity, retention, profit-per-unit, owner (two flavours: attention/focus and hours/capacity) — explicitly labelled "BOS's operationalization of Hormozi's practice, not his verbatim list."
- **C6 (speed-to-lead):** standard = inside 5 minutes during business hours (MIT-backed). Aspiration = 60 seconds via automation. The "4x" figure is directional.
- **C7 (discounting):** "You may design a cheaper offer; you may never cheapen the offer on the table." Ban = reactive same-scope price cuts mid-negotiation. Engineered structures (attraction offers, prepay discounts, scope/payment-changing downsells, continuity discounts, barter-for-reviews) are designed offers. Shape overrides stack (cafes never deep-discount; clinics never price-induce clinical decisions).
- **C8 (premium vs launch-cheap):** launch-cheap = price discovery for a NEW unproven offer, then ratchet using the close-rate table; premium doctrine governs proven offers. Sequence, not conflict.
- **C9 (gross margin bands):** <50% service margin = emergency, Delivery Cube redesign now; 50-80% = below target, plan margin work; ≥80% = healthy. Three-band gauge.
- **C10 (pulse-and-credit-card vs avatar):** take the money at $0-10k; point all DELIBERATE acquisition at one avatar.

## Positive-language resolution (the doctrine sentence)

"Pain is a diagnostic input and a discovery-conversation tool; it never ships in generated customer-facing copy. Where a mechanism structurally names a problem (diagnostic magnets, guarantee conditions), state the condition once, factually, and lead and close with the outcome."

Specific applications: problem lists live in internal worksheets; bonuses state the benefit not the fear; MAGIC Goal components outcome-led; hooks outcome/curiosity-led, contrarian format frames the better way not the reader's failure; ad callouts identity+outcome, never "Tired of X?"; diagnostic lead magnets sell the gain ("Get your borrowing-power score"); upsell deprivation timing is internal, the line names the next win; urgency framed as gain-timing; guarantees state the failure condition once and lead/close on the outcome; live discovery calls MAY name pain (existing memory rule) — cite that boundary so skill authors don't over-sanitize discovery scripts.
