# CLOSER Framework + Obstacles-vs-Objections (targeted mini-sweep)

Sized for founder-led discovery calls in 2-10 person businesses.

## A. CLOSER, step by step

**C — Clarify why they're here.** Get them to state, in their own words, the problem that made them book. Never pitch to a guess. Pattern: "What made you reach out today?" / "What's the goal, and why does it matter right now?" Push past vague answers until a real problem surfaces. *Physio:* "What's the pain stopping you doing that you want back?"

**L — Label the problem.** Play back their situation as a named, confirmed problem, and get agreement before moving on. Pattern: "So it sounds like the real issue is X, and the goal is Y. Is that about right?" *Broker:* "So the fixed rate is ending and you want certainty on repayments before it does. Fair summary?"

**O — Overview past attempts.** Ask what they've already tried, what it cost, and why it didn't stick. Two purposes: empathy, and positioning your solution as a different path rather than "the same thing but better." *Trades:* "Who else has looked at this? What did they say?"

**S — Sell the vacation, not the plane flight.** Pitch the destination (outcome), not the itinerary (features, hours, modules). Device: pick the top three elements of the solution and tie each directly to the outcome they named. *Course creator:* "In 90 days you're publishing weekly and getting paid clients from it," not "12 modules and two calls a month."

**E — Explain away concerns.** Post-pitch objection handling (below). Collaborative, not combative. The buyer needs three beliefs to say yes: they trust you, they believe this solves THEIR problem, and they can access the funds.

**R — Reinforce the decision.** Starts the second they pay: immediate personal onboarding, congratulate the decision, first small win fast. For a small business this is a same-day welcome text/video. Kills buyer's remorse and feeds referrals. Hands off to the activation protocol.

## B. Obstacles vs objections

**The distinction is timing and substance.** An *obstacle* shows up before the ask: a genuine logistical or informational gap (who decides, what it costs, fit). Surface and dismantle obstacles during C-L-O, before the pitch. An *objection* shows up after the ask, and is usually decision-avoidance wearing a costume — the person booked because they already want the problem solved. "The conversation begins with no": most buyers need at least one loop of resistance handled.

**The three buckets (who they blame):**
1. **Circumstances** — money, timing: "can't afford it," "not the right time." Handling: reframe agency. Time: "will you be less busy in three months? We make time for what matters."
2. **Other people** — spouse, partner, boss. Handling: find the real decision-maker early (ideally on the call), and test with "if they said yes, would you?"
3. **Self** — fear and doubt: "need to think about it." Handling: "decisions take information, not time — what's the actual concern?" Then de-risk: guarantee, testimonials, clear next steps.

**Looping (Triple-A):** Acknowledge (repeat the concern back neutrally) → Associate (a story of a past customer with the same hesitation, now glad) → Ask again. Each loop should surface a NEW concern; the same concern recycling is the real one.

**The ethics line:** sell like a coach, not a salesperson — help them make a decision they'd thank you for; never corner. Wrong fit → say so and decline. Stop looping when the concern is a genuine unresolvable obstacle, or stated concerns are resolved and they still say no — take the no gracefully, follow up later.

## C. Adapting notes

- **Clinic consult:** CLOSER structures the conversation about care logistics, never the clinical recommendation. Loop only on schedule/cost-plan, never on "do I need treatment."
- **Trades quote visit:** C-L-O happens on the walkthrough; S is the finished-job picture; one loop max on price, then leave the quote with a clear next step.
- **Discovery/fact-find:** the fact-find IS C-L-O done properly; do those three well and E gets short because obstacles were dismantled before the ask.
