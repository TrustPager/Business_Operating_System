# Set Up Referrals

You install the owner's referral engine: the two levers that actually work are
**results worth talking about** and **an engineered ask**, and the ask is where
most owners leave money on the table. A healthy small business gets a big share of
new work from referrals (§10.7); below that, with happy customers, the ask is
simply missing.

The method and templates live in
[`knowledge/proof-and-referrals-method.md`](../../knowledge/proof-and-referrals-method.md);
the strategy is [`business-method.md`](../../knowledge/business-method.md) §10.7
(the referral engine) and §10.9 (keep it simple for a small business). Read them
before you build.

## Step 0 — Anchor on this owner

Know their trade, who they serve, and how they reach customers (pull from the
profile, or ask one line). This shapes the channel and the reward. Note the
business shape — it drives the reward rules below.

## Step 1 — Design the ask as an introduction, not a request for names

The single highest-leverage change: ask for an **introduction**, not "got any
names?". Write the ask for the owner in their voice, for the moments that matter:
- The three-way intro text (you + the happy client + their contact) — the highest
  converting form.
- The "who do you know who's dealing with [the problem you just solved]?" ask,
  aimed at the specific problem, not a generic "know anyone who needs a [trade]?".

## Step 2 — Time it to the win

The best moment is right after a result lands. If the owner has run
`build-my-proof` and just captured a win, that IS the moment: hand off from the
captured win to the introduction ask while the client is delighted. Otherwise,
name the natural high-emotion moments for their business (job completed, problem
solved, a great review just left) and attach the ask to those.

## Step 3 — Recommend a reward that fits the shape

Make the reward mutual (both the referrer and the new customer get something),
one-click, tracked, and fast-paid. Recommend a specific structure for their
business. **Shape guard — key off `business-method.md` §15's referral &
reward-inducement note (the canonical home):** regulated shapes (finance/mortgage
broking, clinic/health, insurance, legal) can be barred from cash or outcome-tied
referral rewards to unlicensed parties, so for those use a service credit, a
charitable donation, a genuine thank-you, or relationship-only, and tell the
owner to check their regulator. Non-regulated shapes may use cash/credit freely.
Read §15's note rather than guessing, so a newly-added shape inherits the rule.

## Step 4 — Build the tracking sheet

A simple sheet the owner can actually keep: who was asked, who they introduced,
the status (asked / introduced / booked / won), and whether the reward was paid.
Keep it small (§10.9) — a 2-person shop needs a list, not affiliate machinery.

## Step 5 — Hand it over + the connected doorway

Show the scripts, the reward recommendation, and the sheet inline. Offer to save
them (`referrals/referral-plan.md`, the sheet as `.xlsx` via the toolkit if they
want). Then name what deepens when connected, as outcomes: live referral links
that track themselves, automatic reward/commission tracking, and a referral
leaderboard. Reactive, never a cold pitch.

## Output shape — positive-only, no em dashes

The ask scripts are customer-facing: positive-only, no em dashes (commas, colons,
parentheses, separate sentences). Frame the ask around the result the client got
and the help their contact could get, never around what the owner needs.

## Hard rules

- ❌ Never write "got any names?" — the ask is always an introduction to a person
  with a specific problem.
- ❌ No cash referral inducement for a shape that bars it (§15) — use a compliant
  alternative.
- ❌ No multi-tier affiliate machinery for a small owner (§10.9) — a list and a
  simple reward.
- ❌ No em dashes, nothing pain-led, in the customer-facing scripts.
- ✅ Time the ask to a real high-emotion moment; hand off from `build-my-proof`'s
  captured win where there is one.
- ✅ Make the reward mutual and fast-paid; keep the tracking sheet small.
