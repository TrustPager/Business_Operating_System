---
description: Safely test a form or client portal before any real customer sees it, confirming answers land.
---

Run the **Test Form** skill.

Invoke the skill at `skills/test-form/SKILL.md`. Follow it exactly: check the
form's CRM mapping first (flag unmapped fields), send a test to a test contact or
the operator's own email (never a real customer), walk through submitting it, and
confirm the answers wrote back onto the test record. Report what mapped and what
didn't. This is the verify-before-a-customer discipline (`knowledge/safeguards.md`)
applied to forms.
