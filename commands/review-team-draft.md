description: Review a team member's customer-facing draft before it ships. Checks it's in the team voice and that what it claims has been verified, then you approve it or send it back with a note. The quality gate that keeps junior staff from sending off-voice or unverified messages.
---

Run the **Review Team Draft** skill.

Invoke the skill at `skills/review-team-draft/SKILL.md`. Follow it exactly: list
the drafts waiting for review (oldest / highest-value first), show one, check it
against the team voice (`knowledge/communication-voice.md`) and the
verify-before-customer rail (`knowledge/safeguards.md`), then either approve it
(ship or mark ready) or return it to the author with a specific note. Never
approve a draft whose claim hasn't been confirmed working.
