---
description: Turn your content pillars and voice into a dated, ready-to-post calendar for the next week or two.
---

Run the **Plan My Content** skill.

Invoke the skill at `skills/plan-my-content/SKILL.md` and follow it exactly.
Read the brand's `content-pillars.yaml` and `voice.md` first if they exist
(usually under `marketing-strategy/<BrandName>/`, built by
`/build-brand-strategy`). If they're missing, derive 3-4 working pillars from a
short brief rather than blocking.

Keep the horizon clamped to 1-2 weeks (never past 14 days). Every slot names
all five fields: date, channel, pillar, hook, and format. Every hook is
positive and outcome-led, with no em dashes.
