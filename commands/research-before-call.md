---
description: Walk into any meeting prepared, with a one-page brief plus three questions that open the room.
---

Run the **Research Before Call** skill at `skills/research-before-call/SKILL.md`
and follow it exactly.

Pin down one subject first (a company, or a person plus their company). Then
gather signals keylessly with the `firecrawl-scrape` and `firecrawl-search`
skills, scrape and search only, per `knowledge/research-method.md`. Synthesise
the one-page brief in the fixed structure: who they are, what they do, recent
signals, where you connect, and three sharp questions.

One subject per run. If handed a list, do the first and offer the rest one at a
time. Mark inferences as inferences and never invent a signal or a quote.
