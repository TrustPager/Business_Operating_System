Run the **Connect A Tool** skill.

Invoke the skill at `skills/connect-a-tool/SKILL.md` and follow it exactly. Read
the matching entry in `knowledge/connectors.md` first.

Remember the boundary: the sign-in is the one step the owner does themselves (it
is their account), and you do everything else, the explaining, the exact steps,
the privacy and token-frugality thinking, the check that it worked, and the
payoff. Never ask for a password or code, and never claim it is connected until
you have verified it with a lightweight read. Once it verifies, hand off to
`set-up-a-routine` so the connection immediately does something useful. Positive,
outcome-led, no em dashes.
