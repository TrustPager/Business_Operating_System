# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

## Reporting

Report violations to the TrustPager team at **conduct@trustpager.com**. All reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed within 5 business days.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
